11. Key findings and recomendations

| # | Finding |
|---|---------|
| 1 | **Electronics** drives the most revenue (~30% share) with above-average order values |
| 2 | **Q4 (Nov–Dec)** generates ~2× average monthly revenue — strong holiday seasonality |
| 3 | **East region** leads in total revenue; **Central** has the lowest volume |
| 4 | **Enterprise** customers have the highest revenue per customer, despite being only 10% of orders |
| 5 | **20% discounts** are associated with lower avg revenue — deep discounts hurt the bottom line |
| 6 | Revenue and profit grew YoY, with consistent margin stability across years |

1. **Double down on Q4 campaigns** — allocate marketing budget before November to capitalize on peak season
2. **Invest in Enterprise acquisition** — Enterprise CLV is disproportionately high; a dedicated account team would ROI well
3. **Cap discounts at 10%** — revenue data suggests discounts above 10% erode margins without proportional order volume uplift
4. **Expand in Central region** — it underperforms despite similar product mix; investigate pricing and distribution gaps
5. **Protect Electronics margin** — top revenue category; small margin improvements compound significantly
